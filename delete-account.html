1. Başlamadan Önce

Tastick hesabını ve hesabına bağlı verileri istediğin zaman kalıcı olarak silebilirsin. İki yol var: uygulama içinden veya e-posta ile.

Önce aboneliğini iptal et. Tastick Premium aboneliğin varsa, hesabını silmeden önce onu Google Play üzerinden iptal etmen gerekiyor:

Google Play Store → profil resmin → Ödemeler ve abonelikler → Abonelikler → Tastick → Aboneliği iptal et

Hesabını silmek aboneliği kendiliğinden iptal etmez; ödeme almaya devam eder.

2. Yol 1 — Uygulama İçinden (önerilen)

  1. Tastick'i aç ve hesabına giriş yap.
  2. Alt menüden ⚙️ Ayarlar sekmesine geç.
  3. Sayfayı en alta kaydır ve kırmızı Hesabı Sil düğmesine dokun.
  4. Kimliğini doğrulamak için şifreni gir. Google ile giriş yaptıysan Google seni doğrulayacak.
  5. Uyarıyı okuyup onayla.

Silme işlemi anında başlar ve geri alınamaz.

3. Yol 2 — E-posta ile

Uygulamaya erişemiyorsan, hesabına kayıtlı e-posta adresinden oz.studio99@gmail.com adresine "Hesap silme talebi" konusuyla yaz. Talebi 30 gün içinde sonuçlandırıp sana dönüş yaparız.

Not: Talebin, hesabın kayıtlı olduğu e-posta adresinden gelmelidir. Başka bir adresten gelen talepleri, hesabın gerçek sahibini korumak için işleme alamıyoruz.

1. Before You Start

You can permanently delete your Tastick account and all data linked to it at any time. There are two ways: from inside the app, or by email.

Cancel your subscription first. If you have a Tastick Premium subscription, cancel it in Google Play before deleting your account:

Google Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tastick → Cancel subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription by itself; billing continues.

2. Option 1 — In the App (recommended)

  1. Open Tastick and sign in.
  2. Go to the ⚙️ Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap the red Delete Account button.
  4. Enter your password to confirm your identity. If you signed in with Google, Google will verify you.
  5. Read the warning and confirm.

Deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone.

3. Option 2 — By Email

If you can't access the app, email oz.studio99@gmail.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject "Account deletion request". We complete such requests within 30 days.

Note: The request must come from the email address registered to the account. Requests from any other address cannot be processed, in order to protect the real account holder.

4. What Is Deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, profile photo and profile details (bio, company/school, city, LinkedIn, skills)
  • All of your tasks, subtasks, descriptions and routines
  • Photos attached to your tasks
  • Your follows, followers and any pending requests you sent or received
  • Room invitations you sent and received
  • The rooms you own — together with their entire chat history
  • Your membership of every other room
  • Your notification token, storage counter and saved messages
  • Your block list

5. What Is Kept, and Why

DataWhy it staysRetention
Messages and files you posted in someone else's room They are part of that room's shared history; removing them would break the other members' record. After your account is deleted they are no longer linked to your identity. Room messages are deleted automatically after 30 days. If the room owner deletes the room, all of them go at once.

Anonymous crash reports Cannot be linked to your identity; used to fix bugs in the app. 90 days

6. Deleting Only Some Data

If you don't want to delete your whole account, you can delete individual tasks, photos, rooms and messages, and clear your profile fields, from inside the app. No request to us is needed.
